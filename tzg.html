THE EAST MECKLENBURG ZEAGLE GAME MANUAL:

The goal of the game is to accrue the most gigantic score as compared to others. The game is playable on mobile but this is not recommended. The optimal time and place to play this game is on your chromebook during homeroom.

The game, like East Meck, has two major components: Numbers (Math) and Letters (English). Individual numbers stand in as abstract representations of East Meck buildings:

  1. 100 Building
  2. 200 Building
  3. 300 Building
  4. 4000 Building (Remember this is the sterile futuristic cleanroom with big booming fans)
  5. 5000 Building (Remember the inhabiting man. The game contains a clue to why the man lives here)
  6. 600 Building
  7. 700 Building
  8. 800 Building
  9. 900 Building (This is the location of the ZEAGLE headquarters and so has special value in the game)

Zeros can be upgraded into ones via a simple click. Other numbers can also be upgraded into larger numbers via a simple click if the intended number does not exceed the sum of the numbers surrounding the current number (diagonals are not counted).

The extraspecial number nine can be transmogrified into a letter of your choosing by a simple click followed by a key-press (or a clicking of the bottom "click here 2 type" button on mobile). Letters are utilized to construct East Meck and East Meck ZEAGLE related words. This is your main vehicle for accruage of score.

When a valid word is created, you can simply click on said word to create a unique effect dependant upon the word that has been spelt.

The only word which I will tell you about at this time is "hint", which will show you a random other valid word to use. However, the word it will show you will not be valid in the unnatural state in which it is displayed to you. Thus, it must be rebuilt in a more traditional fashion.

Bonuses are handed out for the increase of a number surrounded by numbers that are pairwise equal amongst themselves. Even larger bonuses are handed out for placing the same letter in several places simultaneously.

Passive income can be achieved through the giant booming fans which inhabit the sterile 4000 building. Passive income cannot be achieved through the Space Chancellor though he may help (You cannot take credit for his work). The man in the 5000 also exists.

On (in-game) A-days, all score additions are doubled. On (in-game) B-days, numerical clicks have double the value.

The remainder of the complexity of the game comes from the effects of the vast sea of valid words at your disposal. Valid words can be discovered through use of the word "hint" and pure intuition.
