THE EAST MECKLENBURG ZEAGLE GAME MANUAL:

The goal of the game is to accrue the most gigantic score as compared to others. The game is playable on mobile but this is not recommended. The optimal time and place to play this game is on your chromebook during homeroom with full volume on.

The game, like East Meck, has two major components: Numbers (Math) and Letters (English). Individual numbers stand in as abstract representations of East Meck buildings:

  1. 100 Building
  2. 200 Building
  3. 300 Building (These first three are your bread and butter buildings. Nothing too exciting yet.)
  4. 4000 Building (Remember this is the sterile futuristic cleanroom with big booming fans.)
  5. 5000 Building (Remember the inhabiting man. The game contains a clue to why the man lives here.)
  6. 600 Building (The old and dusty building above the gambling-bunkers.)
  7. 700 Building (You will need to wait for the science update to use this one.)
  8. 800 Building (No special abilities because nobody could find it and go add them.)
  9. 900 Building (This is the location of the ZEAGLE headquarters and so has special value in the game.)

Notice how the 4000/5000/600 triality contains pairwise dualities within. The 400 building does exist in the game but for this building what is important is not what is there, but what is not there.

Zeros can be upgraded into ones via a simple click. Other numbers can also be upgraded into larger numbers via a simple click if the intended number does not exceed the sum of the numbers surrounding the current number (diagonals are never counted).

The extraspecial number nine can be transmogrified into a letter of your choosing by a simple click followed by a key-press (or a clicking of the bottom "click here 2 type" button on mobile). This represents the fact that the ZEAGLE (and its 900 hall headquarters) exists on the boundary between all possible artistic mediums (including literature and mathematics).

Letters are utilized to construct East Meck and East Meck ZEAGLE related words. This is your main vehicle for accruage of score. When a valid word is created, you can simply click on said word to create a unique effect dependant upon the word that has been spelt.

One valid is "hint", which will show you a random other valid word to use. However, the word it will show you will not be valid in the unnatural state in which it is displayed to you. Thus, it must be rebuilt in a more traditional fashion. Many other words are based on East Mecklenburg classes:

  • AP Calculus / IB Analysis (Same effect)
  • AP Literature / IB Literature (...)
  • Economics and Personal Finance (Stock price fluctuation rate displayed)
  • Psychology (Prerequisites: the Space Chancellor and Arrow Keys, mobile devices are not powerful enough for Mind Control)
  • AP World History (Warning: New timelines are a fresh start)
  • AP US History (...)

Letters not part of a valid word cannot typically be removed due to the same reason essays in English class are written in the present tense — literature is eternal.

These numbers and letters form the board — the surface of the game world. The zeros act like an ocean, and everything else acts as land. This board floats upon an expanse of molten metal termed, in analogy with our own planet, the mantle. However, unlike our own planet, actual water is a form of land and gravity acts horizontally.

Bonuses are handed out for the increase of a number surrounded by numbers that are pairwise equal amongst themselves. Even larger bonuses are handed out for placing the same letter in several places simultaneously.

Passive income can be achieved through the giant booming fans which inhabit the sterile 4000 building. Passive income cannot be achieved through the Space Chancellor though he may help (You cannot take credit for his work). The man in the 5000 also exists.

On (in-game) A-days, all score additions are doubled. On (in-game) B-days, numerical clicks have double the value. Being On Time and On Target can be quite profitable but is extremely risky. The 400 split opens up a second view to the mantle on which the board of numbers and letters floats. Clicks are mirrored across the 400 split. Objects along the 400 split are broken and unusable.

The remainder of the complexity of the game comes from the effects of the vast sea of other valid words at your disposal. Valid words can be discovered through use of the word "hint" and pure intuition. Other good starting points are QR Codes and Secret Tunnels.
