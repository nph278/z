The Zeagle Game / The East Meck Game / Crossword Simulator 2026 — Flashing Light Warning

The game is playable on mobile but this is not recommended. The goal of the game is to accrue the most gigantic score as compared to others. The main vehicle for this accruage is the spelling of zeagle-related words followed by the clicking of the initial letter of said word. This will have a different and seperate effect dependent upon the word that is spelt. Post-one numbers are limited by the sum of their neighbors. Diagonal entries are never counted as neighbors. When a number reaches nine it can be transmogrified into a letter by typing physically or virtually by clicking the message "click here 2 type" (mobile). The extra-special word "hint" will provide you the name of another word you can spell for a potential higher degree of profit. However, it will not be usable in the unnatural state it is given to you in and thus must be rebuilt in a more traditional fashion. Bonuses are handed out when new numbers have neighbors that are pairwise equal. Even larger bonuses are handed out for many of the same letter being entered in numerous different locations simultaneously.
